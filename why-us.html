Anyone can say handmade. Here is what it actually looks like.

We are not a factory that uses 'handmade' as a marketing word. We are a kitchen. This is how we work.

Made by people who cook. Not machines that process.

Our kitchen team has decades of combined cooking experience. Every recipe we make was developed the way recipes should be: made, tasted, adjusted, and made again until it was right.

From our kitchen to yours. This is the process.

Step 1 Sourcing

We source premium lentils, whole spices, and dairy directly from trusted markets. Same source, every batch. Consistency in flavour starts before cooking begins.

Step 2 Cooking

Every item is cooked in our kitchen by our team. We do not outsource production. We do not use pre-made bases. Slow-cooked, hand-folded, and made in controlled batches.

Step 3 Quality Check

Before anything is frozen, it is tasted and checked against our batch standard. If it does not match, it does not go out.

Step 4 Freezing & Packaging

We flash-freeze within hours of cooking to lock in texture and flavour. Packaged in premium materials with production date and full ingredient list on every pack.

Step 5 Delivery

Delivered in cold chain packaging. We confirm every order before dispatch to ensure your kitchen receives what it needs, when it needs it.
