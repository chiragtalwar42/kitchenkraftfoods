Created by Chef Karann Talwar

"We built KitchenKraft Foods because we understand the pressure of professional kitchens. Managing consistency at scale is the hardest part of the food business."

With decades of experience in high-end culinary operations, Chef Karann Talwar established KitchenKraft to bridge the gap between volume and quality. Our recipes are developed by chefs, cooked by chefs, and designed specifically to empower your kitchen crew without sacrificing the authentic taste your guests expect.

Handmade means something different here.

Most frozen food is manufactured. Ours is cooked.

Every batch is made by hand in our kitchen, using the same ingredients and the same process every time. No shortcuts that change the taste. No fillers that change the texture.

When your kitchen crew reheats it, it will not taste like it came out of a factory. Because it did not.
