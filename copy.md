How to use this document

Each page is broken into Squarespace blocks. For every block you will see:

HEADLINE: From our kitchen to yours. This is the process.

STEP 1: Sourcing

[suppliers / regions / markets]. Same source, every batch.
Consistency in flavour starts before cooking begins.

STEP 2: Cooking
Every item is cooked in our kitchen by our team.
We do not outsource production. We do not use pre-made bases.
[Add your actual cooking process detail here, e.g. slow-cooked for X hours,
 hand-folded, made in small batches of X kg at a time.]

STEP 3: Quality Check
Before anything is frozen, it is tasted and checked against
our batch standard. If it does not match, it does not go out.
[Add specifics if you have them: temperature checks, weight checks, etc.]

STEP 4: Freezing & Packaging
We flash-freeze within [X hours] of cooking to lock in
texture and flavour. Packaged in [your packaging type]
with production date and full ingredient list on every pack.

STEP 5: Delivery
Delivered in cold chain packaging. [State your delivery radius,
lead time, and minimum order quantity here.]
We confirm every order before dispatch.


NOTE
Fill in your actual numbers. 'Cooked for X hours' is stronger than 'slow cooked.' 'Batches of X kg' is stronger than 'small batches.' Buyers in professional kitchens are detail-oriented. Vagueness reads as hiding something. Specificity reads as confidence.

Phone number is the most important field on this form. A WhatsApp message saying 'Hi [Name], got your sample request for [products]. Can we confirm your address?' converts better than any email. Keep the follow-up personal, fast, and specific to what they asked for.
